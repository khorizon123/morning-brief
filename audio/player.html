Wildfires Force Evacuation of 260,000 in France and Spain as Blazes Spread to Bordeaux Suburbs

France and Spain have extended evacuation orders to roughly 260,000 people as 'unprecedented' wildfires spread, with new orders now covering suburbs within 30km of central Bordeaux. The fires have already forced the mobilization of France's armed forces to assist firefighting efforts.

This is an escalation of the same wildfire crisis reported over the weekend, when about 200,000 people had been evacuated across the two countries.

Flávio Bolsonaro, son of former president Jair Bolsonaro, has been a rallying figure for the Brazilian right; Washington's perceived meddling in Latin American elections has been a recurring flashpoint in the region.

Paramount-Warner Bros. Discovery Deal Frozen Until 2027 as Both Sides Head to Trial

Paramount has agreed to halt its roughly $111 billion acquisition of Warner Bros. Discovery until legal challenges are resolved or June 4, 2027, opting to go straight to a full trial rather than risk losing an August 3 hearing on a court-mandated pause. A dozen Democratic-led states are suing to block the deal on antitrust grounds; Paramount is on the hook for $650 million per quarter in delay payments to WBD starting in October, and a $7 billion breakup fee if the deal collapses over regulatory issues. The standoff is becoming a test case for how much power state attorneys general can wield in antitrust enforcement even as federal regulators wave deals through.

AI IPO Wave Could Trigger $100 Billion Annual Philanthropy Windfall

With OpenAI and Anthropic both expected to launch among the largest IPOs ever, nonprofits and university fundraisers are preparing for a wave of new billionaire donors — potentially $37 billion to $100 billion a year, by one Stripe executive's estimate. Anthropic's seven founders have pledged to give away 80% of their wealth (roughly $90 billion), and OpenAI's nonprofit foundation owns 26% of the company, a stake that could unlock $220 billion in liquid value at IPO. Tax incentives are a major driver too: donating appreciated stock avoids capital-gains bills that can exceed 35% on a sale, and California's proposed one-time 5% billionaire excise tax is pushing some new millionaires toward both giving and reconsidering their state residency. Total 2026 IPO proceeds — including SpaceX's, which minted roughly 4,400 new millionaires — could hit $160 billion, the highest ever recorded.

Company

Humanoid

Humanoid is a London-based robotics startup building humanoid robots for industrial and eventually domestic use, held up by the FT as proof Europe can still compete in advanced manufacturing even as the continent pushes to 'reindustrialise.' It has reached unicorn status despite Europe's broader struggles to match US and Chinese AI investment, positioning itself as a rare European entrant in a field otherwise dominated by companies like Tesla (Optimus) and Figure AI. The company's rise comes as UK premier Andy Burnham signals that chips, drones, and robotics will be central to Britain's industrial strategy. Surprisingly, Humanoid's manufacturing base in Britain runs counter to the assumption that Europe has ceded all hardware manufacturing clout to Asia and the US.4
