G7 Agrees to Release 100m Barrels of Fuel as Trump Drops Export-Ban Threat

The G7 agreed to release 100 million barrels of diesel and crude from strategic reserves, starting immediately and lasting 20 days, prioritizing diesel. In response Trump rescinded his threat to ban US diesel exports, calling the move "a great thing," even as Russia extends its own diesel export ban through October and China suspends October exports.

A squeeze on diesel — tied to Middle East conflict and sanctions on Russian refiners — had pushed US pump prices near record highs and put Washington and allies in crisis-response mode.

US Hiring Slows Sharply, Taking Pressure Off Fed to Raise Rates Again

US employers added just 29,000 jobs in September, far below the 90,000 forecast, with July and August revised down by 60,000 combined. Unemployment ticked up to 4.2% and wage growth slowed to 3% annually — the weakest since the pandemic — softening the case for another Fed rate increase this month.

The Fed had raised rates last month for the first time in three years amid inflation worries from the Iran war and tariffs; a weak jobs report complicates its next move.

A New Industry Emerges: Marketing to AI Shopping Agents

As AI agents from OpenAI and Meta start shopping on people's behalf, a wave of startups — Limy, Gauge, New Generation — are helping brands optimize for bots rather than humans, rewriting product pages into dense, fact-heavy text that agents parse more easily than glossy ad copy. Traffic to US retail sites from AI sources rose 393% in 2026, and Morgan Stanley estimates agent-influenced spend could hit $385bn, or 20% of US e-commerce, by 2030. One cabinetry retailer paid $15,000 to make its site "agent-ready" and plans to spend up to $12,000 a month chasing bot visibility. Techniques range from basic site cleanup to vector-space analysis of which numerical representations of language agents respond to.
