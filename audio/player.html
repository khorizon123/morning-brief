Scientists Warn 'Mirror Life' Research Could Be Catastrophic

Many biological molecules are chiral, existing in one of two mirror-image forms, and a growing number of scientists are exploring whether a cell — likely a bacterium — could be built with its biochemistry entirely reversed. Such an organism could potentially evade immune defenses and ecological checks that have evolved to recognize only 'normal' biochemistry, making it a uniquely dangerous biosecurity risk. Researchers and regulators are being urged to get ahead of the technology now, before it becomes feasible to actually build one, rather than legislating reactively after the fact.

Company

DigitalBridge

DigitalBridge is a data-center and digital-infrastructure investment firm that just struck a $4bn deal to effectively become SoftBank's outside financing arm for AI infrastructure, absorbing capital-intensive buildout costs that would otherwise sit on SoftBank's own balance sheet. The arrangement reflects a broader trend of AI hyperscalers and their backers pushing chip and data-center spending off-balance-sheet through specialized financing vehicles — similar to what Amazon has reportedly explored with Nvidia chips. DigitalBridge CEO Marc Ganzi has positioned the firm as critical plumbing for the AI boom rather than a flashy AI brand itself. Surprisingly, the firm started out in a completely different business — it was originally known as Colony Capital, a real-estate and distressed-debt investor, before pivoting entirely into digital infrastructure.
