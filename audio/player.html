US National Debt Tops $40 Trillion for First Time

America's gross federal debt hit $40.047 trillion, growing $3 trillion in the past year — its fastest pace outside the pandemic. The Treasury responded by doubling its buyback of long-term bonds, briefly calming a bond market where 30-year yields had hit 5.34%, the highest since 2007.

The debt milestone is largely symbolic, but the borrowing binge behind it — driven by military spending, entitlements, and last year's tax cuts — has real consequences: rising bond yields raise costs for mortgages, corporate debt and the government itself.

Global Bond Rout Deepens Before Treasury Buyback Calms Yields

Long-term borrowing costs across major economies hit multi-decade highs this week, with the 30-year US Treasury yield briefly touching 5.34% — the highest since 2007 — before easing to 5.18% after the Treasury announced it would double its long-bond buybacks starting September. UK, German and French long bonds have seen similarly steep climbs.

Investors are pricing in persistent inflation from the Iran war and Hormuz disruptions, plus a wave of AI-infrastructure bond issuance competing with government debt for buyers — a dynamic making borrowing more expensive worldwide.

The tariff standoff has been a running trade-war flashpoint since Trump imposed steep duties on Canadian goods; a deal would mark a rare de-escalation.

Anthropic's Claude Runs Protein-Design Campaigns Largely on Its Own

Anthropic published research showing its Claude models (Mythos Preview and Opus 4.8) autonomously ran drug-discovery protein design campaigns, producing lab-validated working molecules on 14 of 15 targets at success rates of 22-35% — above the industry's typical 10-15%. Twist Bioscience and Adaptyv Bio built and tested the candidates in real labs; separately, Opus 5 analyzed raw instrument data to measure a sample at 96.4% purity in 19 minutes, versus four days for the lab's own report. CEO Dario Amodei had predicted 'early glimmers' in biology within months — this arrived just days later.
