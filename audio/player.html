Canada Declares Trade 'War' With US as Carney Digs In

Prime Minister Mark Carney told Canadians the country is now 'at war' with the US over trade, accusing Trump of miscalculating by escalating the tariff fight, after talks collapsed and Washington imposed 50% tariffs on roughly $20bn of Canadian goods. Canada has vowed to match the tariffs rather than capitulate to US demands over alcohol sales and other trade terms.

This follows a week of on-again, off-again negotiations in which Trump paused and then reimposed the threatened tariffs; Carney's rhetoric marks a sharp escalation in tone from a historically close trading relationship.

US Buyers Snap Up Europe's Asset Managers at Fastest Pace in Decades

American financial giants are acquiring European fund managers at the fastest rate in decades, as struggling local firms find themselves outgunned by larger US rivals racing to bulk up and expand globally. European asset managers have long suffered from fragmentation and higher costs relative to US peers, making them attractive, cheaper targets. The wave adds to a broader story of US capital dominance in global finance even as European regulators and politicians periodically fret about strategic autonomy. Details on specific transactions remain sparse, but the trend marks a structural shift in who controls European retail and institutional savings.

Harvard Business School Deploys AI Video Avatars of Its Professors

Harvard Business School has built AI-generated video avatars of instructors like venture capitalist Jeff Bussgang for a new $699 entrepreneurship bootcamp called HBS Foundry, letting far more founders practice pitches and board meetings than the school's roughly 900 annual MBA students could ever get face time for. Built with avatar-maker HeyGen, the tool simulates video calls where the AI mimics a professor's mannerisms and feedback style; researchers found simply attaching a real professor's photo to a chatbot boosted student trust and engagement. More than 100 universities have piloted the program, with about 760 founders going through it this summer, split between individual payers and university-sponsored access. Faculty say they deliberately tuned the AI to be tough, since large language models tend to default to flattering users.

Company

Blank Street

Blank Street is a six-year-old coffee chain that has just raised fresh funding from General Atlantic at a roughly $650 million valuation, as it tries to position itself as a 'daily luxury brand' and expand into ice cream. Founded in 2020, the chain built its identity around small, efficient grab-and-go kiosks rather than sprawling cafes, letting it scale quickly across cities like New York, London and Boston. It's interesting now because it's one of the few consumer food-and-beverage startups still attracting serious late-stage private equity money in a tighter funding environment for retail concepts. The surprising fact: despite its premium, minimalist branding, Blank Street's kiosk-first model was originally conceived as a low-capex way to compete with Starbucks without needing full-sized stores.
