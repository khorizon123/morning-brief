Swiss Voters to Decide Fate of Centuries-Old Neutrality

Switzerland holds a referendum on whether to bar the country from cooperating with military alliances unless directly attacked, a vote that could upend one of Europe's oldest foreign-policy traditions. A 'Yes' would cement strict neutrality into law just as European states rearm amid the Ukraine war and rising Russian hybrid aggression.

Swiss neutrality dates to the 19th century and has kept the country out of NATO and EU defense structures; the vote comes as European governments debate how far to integrate militarily against Russia.

The chasm between AI companies' sky-high valuations and their actual revenue is becoming the defining feature of the next wave of AI listings. Company

Nscale

Nscale is a British AI cloud provider racing toward a $35bn New York IPO, positioning itself as neutral infrastructure for training and running large AI models — essentially renting out GPU-packed data centers to labs that don't want to build their own. Its S-1 filing prominently features marquee contracts with Microsoft and Anthropic, but as recently reported, it curiously omits any prominent mention of ByteDance (TikTok's parent), even though the Chinese tech giant has been one of its biggest early customers. The company is emblematic of a new tier of AI infrastructure players trying to cash in on the compute boom without directly building foundation models themselves. Its valuation ambitions put it in the same league as some of the AI labs it serves, despite being far less known to the public.
