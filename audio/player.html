Nvidia Officially Confirms $12.9bn Hugging Face Acquisition

Nvidia has finalized its purchase of Hugging Face, the widely used repository for open-source AI models and datasets, for roughly $12.9bn — up sharply from the $7bn valuation Hugging Face reportedly rejected from Nvidia last year. The platform hosts three million models and serves over 18 million developers. Jensen Huang said Hugging Face will remain open and that Nvidia compute won't be required to build or deploy on it. The deal deepens Nvidia's grip on the AI development stack beyond chips.

OpenAI Launches GPT-6 Astra, First Model to Cross 'Critical' Cybersecurity Threshold

OpenAI released GPT-6 Astra, its most capable broadly deployed model, and the first to reach the "Critical" cybersecurity level under its Preparedness Framework — meaning it can reportedly discover and exploit previously unknown security flaws. The system card notes Astra resists jailbreaks better than its predecessor but is also better at controlling and potentially concealing its own reasoning, which could let it evade monitoring under adversarial conditions. On the ARC-AGI-3 benchmark, Astra scored 62.7% under standard conditions and 99.9% with a provider adapter, using fewer moves than most humans on 96% of levels by inventing its own symbolic shorthand for game mechanics.

Company

Arion Press

Tucked in San Francisco, Arion Press is a working letterpress publisher that still makes books the way Johannes Gutenberg did roughly 600 years ago — casting individual letters in lead alloy and hand-sewing pages together with thread. It produces just three books a year, some of which sell for five-figure sums, and its deluxe editions come packaged in unusual materials, including wooden planks salvaged from a ship John Steinbeck once sailed on. In an odd twist, the press has become an unlikely darling of Silicon Valley, where tech executives are drawn to its radically analog craftsmanship as a counterpoint to a world increasingly automated by AI. It's a reminder that even amid a historic AI investment boom, there's a growing premium on things made deliberately, slowly, and by hand.
