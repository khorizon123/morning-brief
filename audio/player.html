Strait of Hormuz Reopening Nears as US Stocks Hit Record High

Iranian and Omani negotiators have agreed on a temporary arrangement to route shipping through the Strait of Hormuz — inbound vessels through Iranian waters, outbound through a channel near Oman — though Iran's top leadership still must sign off. Treasury Secretary Scott Bessent said a deal could come within a day or two, and the S&P 500 hit a record high on the news, though officials in Washington and Tehran still disagree over fees and other details, and a new strike hit a cargo vessel in the strait overnight.

The US and Israel attacked Iran in February, and the strait — a chokepoint for roughly a fifth of the world's oil — has been the main battleground since, with Iran blockading it for months.

Apple Seeks Injunction to Block OpenAI's Device Push Over Alleged Trade Secrets

Apple asked a US judge for a preliminary injunction barring OpenAI and two former Apple employees, Chang Liu and Tang Yew Tan, from using its alleged trade secrets in OpenAI's hardware unit, io Products. Company

Simile

Simile is a year-old Stanford-linked startup that builds AI agents designed to simulate how specific groups of people — equity analysts, shoppers, brand audiences — will react to a product, message, or policy, effectively running focus groups without any actual humans in the room. It just raised $200 million at a $2 billion valuation led by Greenoaks, an eye-popping jump for a company still finding its footing in market research and consumer insights. The pitch is that companies can now test ad campaigns, pricing, or public messaging against thousands of AI-simulated "personas" before spending a dollar on real focus groups. The surprising bit: its "agentic twins" have been used to model reactions from niche, hard-to-reach populations — like Wall Street equity analysts — a group traditionally almost impossible to survey at scale. Its rapid rise underscores how fast venture money is flowing into AI applications that go beyond chatbots and into synthetic simulations of human behavior itself. ​
