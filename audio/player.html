Kushner Meets Hamas and Netanyahu in New Push to Revive Gaza Deal

Jared Kushner met with Hamas leaders in Egypt and was set to meet Netanyahu on Monday, a week after Israel publicly rejected Trump's 15-point Gaza peace plan. Israel now controls roughly 60% of the Strip, and hardline minister Itamar Ben-Gvir has called for Israel to kill "30 to 40" Gazans nightly and rebuild settlements there. Hamas urged mediators to "compel the occupation" to meet its ceasefire obligations.

Trump Orders Reduced Military Drills With South Korea, Citing Ties With Kim

Trump ordered the Pentagon to "substantially reduce" the Ulchi Freedom Shield exercises with South Korea, which began Monday with roughly 18,000 South Korean troops, citing his "very good relationship" with Kim Jong Un and Seoul's non-involvement in the Iran war. The drills, which simulate GPS disruption, cyber-attacks and drone warfare, are set to run 11 days.

The annual US-South Korea exercises are meant to deter North Korea; scaling them back marks an unusual gesture toward Pyongyang from a sitting US president.

US-Iran Talks Collapse Again as 60-Day Deadline Passes; Hormuz Traffic Still a Trickle

The US and Iran missed the 60-day deadline set under June's ceasefire to reach a broader deal. Shipping through the Strait of Hormuz slowed to just five vessels over the weekend versus 31 the prior weekend, per Kpler data, with Brent crude now around $88-89, some $16 above pre-war levels. Iran's supreme leader has installed hardline commanders in top security roles, signaling no near-term compromise.

A fifth of the world's oil and LNG normally transits Hormuz; the prolonged closure is a key driver of elevated global energy prices amid the broader Iran conflict.

US Deportation Deals Create Overseas 'Indefinite Detention' Archipelago

The Trump administration is paying at least 35 countries — including Eswatini, South Sudan, Ghana and Moldova — to hold deportees from third countries, often with no legal recourse or charges. One case: Roberto Mosquera, a Cuban-born plumber deported after 45 years in the US despite serving his sentence long ago, has been held over a year in Eswatini's maximum-security prison with no path to release.

Unlike traditional deportation to a person's home country, these arrangements send migrants to nations with no connection to them, effectively creating stateless prisoners beyond US court jurisdiction.

Growth had been 1.8% in Q1. Separately, Japan's 10-year bond yield hit a three-decade high near 3% as a weak yen fuels inflation concerns and bets on a rate rise next month.

Beijing Neurosurgeon Cracks 22-Year-Old Math Problem Using GPT-5.6

Shanmu Jin, a Beijing neurosurgery resident with no formal advanced math training, proved Crouzeix's Conjecture — an unsolved matrix problem since 2004 — after a 16-hour autonomous session with OpenAI's GPT-5.6 Sol, using a technique that pits AI subagents against each other with no internet access. Cornell mathematician Alex Townsend and the conjecture's original author have verified the proof, though formal peer review is pending. It's the latest sign AI is producing real mathematical breakthroughs from non-experts, shifting the scarce skill toward verification rather than discovery.
