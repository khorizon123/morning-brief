Mike Ashley's Frasers Group

Frasers Group, the British conglomerate built by billionaire Mike Ashley from his bargain sportswear chain Sports Direct, has been on a luxury buying spree — this week revealing a 48% stake in Germany's Hugo Boss, just short of outright control, days after acquiring the upmarket department store Harvey Nichols and disclosing a 4% stake in Burberry. It's a jarring pivot for a company whose founder holds business meetings in pubs, challenges subordinates to drinking contests, and once reportedly vomited into a fireplace after downing 12 pints. Ashley left school at 16 with a single C-grade qualification, dreamed of being a professional squash player, and started his first shop with a £10,000 remortgage from his parents. He's since built a reputation as a bruiser dealmaker — famously calling investors who lost money after a 2007 share sale 'cry babies' — and now finds himself, improbably, one of Europe's most aggressive buyers of high fashion.mikeashley.com=frasersgroup.com.
