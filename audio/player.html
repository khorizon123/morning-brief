Iran War Has Cost US $38bn So Far, CBO Estimates

The Congressional Budget Office says the conflict with Iran has cost the Pentagon $38 billion since it began, with an additional $2-3bn a month projected going forward, not counting damaged base repairs. The office estimates it will take at least five years to replace missile interceptors expended defending US troops.

The war has been running since February and has already driven up global oil prices and US fuel costs by tens of billions of dollars for consumers.

Company

Base Power

Base Power, founded in 2023 by Zach Dell (son of Michael Dell), installs household batteries that double as active grid assets, discharging stored power back to the grid during peak demand to profit from arbitrage. The company just closed a $1bn funding round valuing it at $13bn, capitalizing on the AI-driven surge in electricity demand straining US grids. Unlike most billionaire heirs who buy sports teams or vanity projects, Dell has bounced between ventures — a dating app called Thread and a developing-world sanitation startup called Sqwatt — before landing on grid batteries. He now claims 'the biggest company in the world in a decade could very likely be a power company.'
