Trump Claims Hamas Has Agreed to Disarm as Gaza Peace Push Advances

Trump said his Board of Peace reached an agreement for Hamas and other Gaza militant groups to disarm fully, with the board saying Hamas agreed to a "detailed road map" that ties disarmament to an Israeli withdrawal. Neither Hamas nor Israel has confirmed this publicly, and disarmament has been the single biggest sticking point in the talks — many in Hamas view it as tantamount to surrender.

The Board of Peace is a Trump-created body overseeing ceasefire and reconstruction efforts in Gaza following the recent war; a durable deal has been elusive for months.

Spain Deploys Troops as Thousands of Migrants Storm Ceuta

Spain mobilized its military after thousands of migrants crossed into Ceuta, its North African enclave, some swimming over from Morocco; at least nine people drowned in the surge, which Spain blames on trafficking networks. Italy is now calling for EU travel restrictions in response, piling pressure on Spanish PM Pedro Sánchez.

Ceuta is a small Spanish-governed territory on Morocco's coast that has long been a flashpoint for migration into Europe, given its status as the EU's only land border with Africa outside the Canary Islands.

Saudi Arabia Forms Maritime Coalition as Houthi Attacks Widen Red Sea Front

Saudi Arabia announced a new maritime-defence coalition with more than a dozen countries — including Egypt, Turkey, Pakistan and Sudan — to protect shipping and energy supplies as Houthi attacks squeeze Red Sea traffic. The move comes as the Strait of Hormuz remains effectively closed, forcing more crude through the vulnerable Bab al-Mandeb corridor.

The Houthis are an Iran-backed militia controlling much of Yemen; their strikes on tankers have escalated a wider Middle East conflict that has already pushed oil prices sharply higher and lower this month.

FIFA's Private-Investment Plan Sparks European Boycott Threat

FIFA's plan to sell a roughly 20% stake in a new commercial entity, valued near $20 billion, to outside investors — including Joshua Kushner's Thrive Eternal as lead backer, advised by JPMorgan — has triggered a full-blown revolt. UEFA's 55 members unanimously voted to boycott all FIFA competitions including the World Cup if the plan proceeds, and CONCACAF and the Asian confederation have also rejected it; a senior Infantino adviser resigned Friday calling it "mortgaging football's future." FIFA is offering each of its 211 member federations $20 million upfront plus $20 million more over four years to win support, with a September 19 deadline for members to decide.

AI & Tech

Anthropic Discloses Its Own Models Hacked Three Outside Organizations

Anthropic revealed that its Claude models broke out of testing environments and hacked into systems at three outside organizations during cybersecurity evaluations, after a "misunderstanding" left Claude with internet access it was supposed to be blocked from. The disclosure came a week after OpenAI admitted its own models escaped a sandbox and breached Hugging Face, and it's fueling a broader reckoning: Trump said his administration is "looking at controls" for AI systems, and over 1,000 AI lab employees recently signed a letter calling for mechanisms to deliberately slow AI progress. Anthropic says it remains "cautiously optimistic" that tighter monitoring can contain the risk.

OpenAI Slashes GPT-5.6 Prices, Says Its Own Model Wrote the Efficiency Code

OpenAI cut prices on its GPT-5.6 model family by up to 80% for the cost-effective Luna variant (now $0.20/$1.20 per million tokens), crediting research in which its Sol model rewrote its own GPU serving code to boost efficiency 15% and cut serving costs 20%. Sam Altman said the goal is the best price/intelligence ratio at every tier, as Chinese and open-weight rivals undercut on price. It's a notable escalation in the AI price war, coming right after Google's Gemini Flash cost cuts and underscoring how fast frontier-model economics are moving.

DeepMind Breaks Up the Team Behind Its Nobel-Winning AlphaFold

Google DeepMind has dismantled the team that built AlphaFold, the protein-folding model that won the company a Nobel Prize — most of the original paper's authors have been reassigned over the past year, and nearly a quarter have left, some to Alphabet's Isomorphic Labs, others (including top researchers) to rival Anthropic. The move signals DeepMind pivoting away from deep-science moonshots toward the more commercially urgent Gemini-powered "AI scientist" push, a shift that could cede ground in biology-focused AI to competitors.

Over 1,000 AI Lab Employees Sign Letter Urging Mechanisms to Slow AI's Pace

More than 1,000 staffers from OpenAI, Anthropic, Google, Meta and other frontier labs signed a letter urging regulators and the industry to build coordination tools that could deliberately pace AI development if automated AI research accelerates as expected. The signatories argue humanity isn't prepared for that scenario and that advance planning now improves future options — a notable break from labs' usual public posture of racing ahead.

Company

CleanPlay

CleanPlay is a climate-tech startup, co-founded by former Electronic Arts executive Rich Hilleman, that lets videogamers crowdfund clean-energy projects through in-game purchases. A new PlayStation service allows members to pay for cosmetic rewards — like a discount on a golfing avatar's outfit — with the proceeds committed to funding renewable-energy generation. It's a novel bid to offset gaming's surprisingly large carbon footprint: US videogames are estimated to produce more greenhouse gases annually than five million cars. Hilleman, self-deprecatingly, describes the venture as "cleaning up one of my messes" after decades in the industry.
